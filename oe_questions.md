immunotherapy drug. For each, is every clinical claim in the quoted lines
correct as stated? If a number or statement is wrong, give the correct one.

The oxygen will feed the fire before they scope or cut, so warn them, keep the flow down low." Is 400 units cumulative the accepted risk threshold for bleomycin pulmonary toxicity, and is peri-procedural high FiO2 an established precipitant that should be limited?
