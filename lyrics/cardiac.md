# Drug-Induced Cardiac Toxicity

Song 2 of the chemo/immunotherapy set. Nine verses. Checkpoints deliberately
last (verse 9) so they do not share a slot with the lung song's verse 5.

Status: rendered in Suno twice, as celtic folk punk and as Irish rebel;
Grey keeps whichever enunciates better. Not yet checked in OpenEvidence. The chat itself flagged the doxorubicin threshold and the
checkpoint-myocarditis mortality as numbers to verify. Open questions are in
`oe_questions.md`.

Style:

    celtic folk punk stomp, single clear-diction male lead vocal, dry close-mic
    vocal with no backing vocals, fiddle and banjo and accordion, strummed
    acoustic guitar, upright bass, driving four-on-the-floor kick, 140 BPM, one
    syllable per note, vocals every bar, consistent full-band energy

[Verse 1]
The doxorubicin will scar
the muscle, slow and deep
Two-fifty marks the modern line
no dose you give is cheap
The first year is when most will show
not decades down the line
The echo tells you when it starts
the E.F. in decline

[Verse 2]
Trastuzumab attacks the same
the E.F. starts to fall
The parent takes the heart, not lung
the payload takes them all
But stop the drug and give it time
the E.F. will come back
So echo first, then echo through
and keep the heart on track

[Verse 3]
The five-F.U. will clamp the flow
a spasm, not a clot
Capecitabine does the same
the chest pain comes on hot
It hits them while the drug still runs
not days or weeks away
The troponin may still be clear
so hold the drug today

[Verse 4]
Carfilzomib will fail the heart
in cycle one, the worst
The myeloma patient swells
the B.N.P. comes first
It looks like simple overload
but that is not the source
So order up an echo now
and hold the drug's next course

[Verse 5]
Ibrutinib will bring the fib
and bleeding, side by side
The rhythm breaks, the pressure climbs
a difficult divide
You want to thin the blood for stroke
but bleeding is the cost
So check the rhythm every time
or else the beat is lost

[Verse 6]
The arsenic will stretch Q.T.
and nilotinib too
Osimertinib does the same
ondansetron runs through
Together they can stack the risk
so fix the lytes today
So check the E.K.G. each time
or torsades leads the way

[Verse 7]
Sunitinib and sorafenib
lenvatinib as well
Bevacizumab does the same
and blood pressure will swell
It marks the drug is working right
so treat it, do not stop
But watch for clots and failing heart
when pressure hits the top

[Verse 8]
Ponatinib will clot the line
the arteries, not veins
A stroke, an M.I., limb that dies
that's what the box explains
Nilotinib and dasatinib
can do the vessel harm
So check the pulses, check the legs
and sound the right alarm

[Verse 9]
The checkpoints hit the heart as well
it's rare, it comes on fast
The first two doses, weeks not months
and one in three won't last
The ipi-nivo lifts the risk
near five times over one
The E.F. can be normal still
the heart block gets it done

[End]
