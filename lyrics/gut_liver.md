# Drug-Induced Gut and Liver Toxicity

Song 3 of the chemo/immunotherapy set. Seven verses, drug first in every
verse. Verse slots chosen against the other two songs: checkpoints are
lungs 5 and cardiac 9, so colitis is verse 1 and hepatitis verse 4;
busulfan is lungs 7, so VOD is verse 5.

Status: drafted 2026-09-17, not rendered, not checked in OpenEvidence.
Questions 17 to 23 in `oe_questions.md`.

Style:

    sea shanty, 6/8 stomp at 100 BPM, single clear-diction male lead vocal,
    dry close-mic vocal, accordion, fiddle, bodhran, strummed acoustic guitar,
    one syllable per note, vocals every bar, consistent full-band energy

[Verse 1]
The ipi burns the colon raw
the stool count climbs all day
So send for C. diff, C.M.V.
then steroids, don't delay
If three days pass and nothing turns
infliximab is due
or vedolizumab will do
don't let the scope stall you

[Verse 2]
Irinotecan hits the gut
in two ways, fast and slow
The early kind is cramps and sweat
so atropine will go
The late kind comes a week or more
with fever and low counts
High-dose loperamide at home
admit when fever mounts

[Verse 3]
Capecitabine strips the gut
the mouth, the hands, the stool
But if it's savage, early, all
then D.P.D.'s the rule
There is an antidote, but fast
four days from the last dose
It's uridine triacetate
so page; the clock is close

[Verse 4]
The nivo lifts the liver tests
the A.L.T. will climb
So rule out virus, rule out drugs
grade two, it's steroid time
No infliximab when they fail
it hurts the liver more
It's mycophenolate you add
and biopsy for sure

[Verse 5]
The busulfan that preps the graft
will clog the liver's veins
Inotuzumab does it too
with weight gain, jaundice, pains
It comes within the first three weeks
the liver's big and sore
Defibrotide is what you give
so call the transplant floor

[Verse 6]
Cytarabine will wreck the wall
with neutrophils at none
Right lower pain and fever both
typhlitis has begun
So C.T., no colonoscope
the wall could tear and bleed
Antibiotics, rest the gut
and surgeons if they need

[Verse 7]
Asparaginase clots the veins
the pancreas as well
So lipase, sugar, bili, clot
the four that always tell
In A.L.L. the young get this
the belly pain is true
A sinus thrombosis can strike
a headache's imaged too

[End]
