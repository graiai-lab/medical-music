# The Cranial Nerves

Anatomy song 1. The control experiment for the chemo set: same 8-6-8-6
meter, item first in every couplet pair, but vocabulary Grey already
knows. If recall is good here and poor on the drug songs, the drug names
are the problem, not the format. Two nerves per verse, four lines each:
number and name, foramen, function, bedside sign.

[Verse 1]
Olfactory is number one
the cribriform's the gate
A blow up front will shear it off
and smell has met its fate
The optic nerve is number two
the optic canal's door
It carries sight and pupil in
so swing the light once more

[Verse 2]
Oculomotor, number three
the fissure lets it through
It lifts the lid, it turns the eye
and pinches pupils too
When down and out with drooping lid
a wide one means it's pressed
The trochlear, four, aims down and in
so stairs become the test

[Verse 3]
Trigeminal is number five
the face in three it feels
The fissure, then rotundum, then
ovale, chewing meals
Abducens is the number six
it pulls the eye out wide
When pressure in the skull is high
the first to fail, cross-eyed

[Verse 4]
The facial nerve, the seventh, moves
the face, and tastes the front
It exits stylomastoid's hole
the forehead's where you hunt
If forehead's spared, it's from the brain
if weak, the nerve's the source
The eighth one hears and keeps you straight
the meatus, same course

[Verse 5]
Glossopharyngeal is nine
the back of tongue it knows
Through jugular it feels the throat
the gag: that's where it goes
The vagus, ten, goes down below
through jugular as well
The uvula will point away
and hoarse is how they tell

[Verse 6]
Accessory, eleven, shrugs
and turns the head aside
Through jugular, the third of three
trapezius beside
Hypoglossal, twelve, moves the tongue
its own canal, its door
The tongue will point toward the side
where lesion lies, for sure

[End]
